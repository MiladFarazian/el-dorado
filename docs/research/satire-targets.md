## 1. TEXAS-SPECIFIC TARGETS (with mid-2026 receipts)

### The Grid: a Failure Ritual with Seasons
- Real: ERCOT forecasts demand nearly DOUBLING to ~175 GW in six years; 438,000 MW of connection requests sit in queue, ~90% data centers. Summer 2026 peak projected at a record 92.2 GW. Three escalating alert tiers (Weather Watch → Voluntary Conservation → Conservation Appeal) that Texans now treat like liturgical seasons.
- The joke: the only grid in America that seceded from the other grids, asks citizens to unplug their fridges so server farms can finish a chatbot's homework, and calls this "independence." EIA projects a 79% price hike by 2027 driven by data centers.
- Game ritual: seasonal "Conservation Appeal" events — streetlights die, NPCs grill on lawns at 8 p.m., the rich neighborhoods hum with generator noise. Radio proudly reports "the grid performed as designed" during the blackout.

### The AI Data-Center Land Rush
- Real: Texas on track to have more data centers than anywhere on Earth by 2030; 49 BILLION gallons of water consumed by them in 2026 alone, up to 399B/yr by 2030 (~6.6% of state water). A former governor's firm is building 18M sq ft of data centers + four private nuclear reactors next to a university in the Panhandle. Server barns land next to subdivisions whose residents can't get reliable tap water.
- The joke: the Cattle Baron reincarnated as a REIT. Land men knocking on farm doors again — this time for "compute." Towns get 40 permanent jobs and a substation the size of the town.
- Game presence: **GigaStead** compute ranches on the map's edges, humming at 72 dB, ringed by "ECONOMIC MIRACLE IN PROGRESS" banners; a mission line where a town's wells go dry the same week GigaStead's koi pond is filled.

### Crypto Mining Ranches
- Real: Granbury residents (Hood Co.) have THREE active suits against a Bitcoin miner over 24/7 fan noise — insomnia, tinnitus, vibrations through house walls; they tried to incorporate as a city just to pass a noise ordinance and lost the vote. Consolidated federal cases before a judge in 2026.
- The joke: the company's decibel meter always reads exactly one point below the legal limit. Punch UP: the retirees with clipboards are the heroes; the villain is the mine's PR guy calling the noise "the sound of freedom."

### HQ Gold Rush & Yeehaw Capital
- Real: DFW led the U.S. with 111 HQ relocations 2018–2025; relocation activity up 70% YoY. The Texas Stock Exchange ("Y'all Street") went LIVE in Dallas July 2026, backed by BlackRock/Citadel/Goldman; NYSE and Nasdaq both opened Texas outposts to compete.
- The joke: VCs in $900 ostrich boots that have never touched dirt, saying "Texas is the new Rome" into a podcast mic inside a former Cheesecake Factory. Three stock exchanges racing to out-Texas each other.
- Game: financial district **"Howdy Street"** anchored by **The Texchange**; recruiters outside offering relocation bonuses to anyone in a Patagonia vest.

### Master-Planned Megasprawl
- Real: Celina grew 24.6% in ONE YEAR (fastest in America); 1,380-acre communities with 4,000+ homes; "build-to-rent" subdivisions; a 190-acre Walmart-anchored "mixed-use lifestyle district."
- The joke: communities named for the nature they bulldozed (The Groves at Prairie Song — no groves, no prairie, HOA-mandated song). Homes "from the $400s," trees from 2041. Amenity barn. Lifestyle director.
- Game: **Harvest Hills™ by Pioneer Vision Development** — an endless half-built frontier of framing lumber, model homes, and cul-de-sacs that dead-end into cow pasture.

### High-Speed Rail Purgatory
- Real: Dallas–Houston bullet train announced 2014, construction "starting" since 2021, opening now "early 2030s"; federal grant yanked 2025, Japanese and Spanish investors quietly wrote it all off. Still officially "alive."
- The joke: the train exists only as renderings and press conferences. Game bit: a gleaming **Pecos Bullet** sales office (est. 2013) staffed by one aging true believer; billboard countdown clock reading "ARRIVING IN: 7 YEARS" that never changes. NPCs use it as a unit of time ("that'll get done when the train does").

### Border Theater (institutional absurdity only)
- Real: Operation Lone Star at $11 BILLION and counting; 70,000 rolls of razor wire, buoy barriers, 100 miles of UNCONNECTED wall projected to take 30 more years and $20B; state now billing the feds for reimbursement.
- The joke: the contractors, the invoices, the press-conference-per-mile ratio. Satirize **Operation Longhorn Shield** as a jobs program for buoy manufacturers and a governor's photo-op pipeline. Migrants are never the joke; the procurement process is.

### THC Gray Market Whiplash
- Real: 2025 ban (SB 3) passed → VETOED after veteran outcry → two special sessions failed → executive-order regulation; most smokables banned in 2026, retailer fees jumped $150 → $5,000; Congress then banned most hemp THC federally in a shutdown deal. An $8B Texas industry built entirely on a farm-bill typo.
- The joke: the most freedom-loving state criminalizing a gummy while every gas station still sells them from behind a curtain. Game: **Smoke 'Em If You Got 'Em** vape barns flipping signage weekly ("NOW LEGAL" / "NEVER MIND" / "ASK BUBBA").

### Sports Betting Purgatory
- Real: still illegal in 2026; a casino giant's PAC spent millions on primaries and LOST — further from legalization than 2023; meanwhile World Cup betting surges through "prediction market" loopholes on every phone.
- The joke: you can't bet on the Rustlers in Texas, but the app will let you "buy a YES contract" on the same game. Everyone's uncle is a "forecaster" now.

### Megachurch Industrial Complex
- Real: a Southlake megachurch founder pled guilty (2025) to child sex abuse; the institution then generated a defamation suit, an insurance company suing to avoid paying, a tithing-fraud suit dismissed on First Amendment grounds, and a fight over the disgraced founder's multimillion retirement payout.
- Punch-up line: the ABUSE IS NEVER A JOKE and survivors are never on screen as material. The target is the machine around it: the PR statements, the jet, the elder board, the seed-faith economics, the retirement package negotiated before the apology.
- Game: **Overflow Fellowship** (12 campuses, arena rigging, sermon series with movie-trailer marketing, tithing app with streak flames).

### HOA Authoritarianism
- Real: Texas has NO statewide cap on HOA fines; associations can foreclose over unpaid assessments; 2025 reforms had to legislate basics like "a hearing before the fine."
- Game: the **Stonebridle Ranch HOA** as a faction — drone patrols measuring grass height, "approved beige" paint audits, a president with a lanyard and a dream. Side missions: liberate a flamingo lawn ornament.

### The $100K Pickup Arms Race
- Real: top trims now crest six figures (Rivian Quad $115,990; Hummer EV $107,995); King Ranch ~$72K, Denali Ultimate ~$83K. Beds that have never carried anything heavier than a Costco run.
- Game truck: **Steed Longrider "Baron Brisket" Edition** — massaging seats, bed-mounted griddle, 9 mpg, financed over 96 months.

### Water Wars
- Real: the Marvin Nichols reservoir fight — DFW wants a $7B lake that would drown ~70,000 acres of East Texas timber and farmland; state board declared a formal "interregional conflict" in 2025, mediation failed into a binding decision, plan adopted January 2026 with the reservoir still in it. A literal city-vs-country land war, adjudicated by acronyms.
- Game: the **Lake Reckoning** project — survey stakes appearing on NPC farms, town-hall shouting matches, a slick suburban water authority ("Your Lawn Matters™").

### Bonus DFW-current
- **Transit secession**: Plano, Irving & suburbs voting through 2026 on quitting the DART-alike (**MART**) right before the World Cup — "Now Serving 40% Fewer Cities."
- **Universal Kids Resort** opened July 1, 2026 in Frisco → **Colossal Kidz Resort, Brisco** — a theme park for toddlers priced like a mortgage.

## 4. PUNCH-UP RULES (binding)
1. **Targets**: institutions, industries, hype cycles, money, power, PR machines. The grid operator, not the linesman. The megachurch's jet, not the congregation's faith — and abuse/victims are NEVER material; the satire is the cover-up machinery, the payouts, the statements.
2. **Never targets**: ordinary people, victims, migrants, protected groups. Granbury-style residents, retail workers, rural landowners, congregants, transit riders = sympathetic; often the heroes.
3. **Border satire** = procurement, contractors, photo-ops, invoices, unconnected wall miles. Zero jokes at migrants' expense; they appear, if at all, as people the theater is performed around, with dignity.
4. **No real living person as a character.** Composite archetypes only (Colt Bidwell, Pastor Everly, Buck Sterling). No real names, likenesses, or one-to-one biography mapping; every composite draws from ≥3 sources plus invention.
5. **True crime**: all in-game cases fictional; the target is the content economy, not any real case or victim.
6. **Texans are in on the joke, not the butt of it.** The tone is a Texan roasting Texas — HollerBurger is beloved AND absurd. Affection is what separates satire from sneering, and it's why Rockstar's LA works.
7. **Both barrels rule**: every ideology on the dial gets roasted (Patriot Talk AND Megaplex Public Radio). The satire is of certainty, grift, and hype — wherever they live.
8. **Specific beats broad**: a $87 toll fee, a 96-month truck loan, a beaver-mascot restroom empire. If a joke could run in any city, sharpen it until it could only run here.
