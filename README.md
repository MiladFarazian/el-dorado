# EL DORADO GRANDE — an open-world crime-satire game set in the Megaplex

**Play:** open `game/project.godot` in Godot and press F6 on `main.tscn`, or F5
to run the project. The opening screen selects **Hook and Ladder** at Longhorn
Dispatch. Enter the city and follow the gold radar route. Stop in the dispatch
marker to begin the job. **M / Escape** opens the paused city map, activities,
and controls. **E** exits/enters cars; **F** operates the tow hook.

**Latest work (2026-09-06):** opening/pause flow, selectable city map, downtown
street navigation, live mission guidance, and pause integration checks. This
is still a playable prototype; the milestone history below is not a claim of
finished GTA-level visual quality. See `docs/qa/session-flow.md` for evidence
and current limits.

An original open-world action game with GTA 5/6 genre DNA, set in **the Megaplex** — a fictionalized Dallas–Fort Worth. Two downtowns with opposite souls (glass **Dorado**, brick **Fort Verde**), a Manhattan-sized airport between them, frontage-road chase grammar, supercell skies, and a 2026 boom economy that satirizes itself: data centers drinking the aquifer, "Y'all Street," PE landlords, megachurch empires.

**The story:** Booker "Book" Reyes — ex-bull-rider turned repo man — discovers the land flips keeping his family afloat are clearing his own neighborhood for a data-center campus, and turns his tow hook on the boom. Four verbs: **the Hook** (wrecker), **the Saddle** (horse), **the Slow Lane** (slab culture — slow is the flex), **the Paper** (title fraud as heist). The weather calendar is the act structure; the sky is the boss fight.

Built by Milad + a team of AI agents. Currently in **pre-production** (discovery complete, 2026-07-29).

## Run it
```sh
cd game && /Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot
```
**Driving:** WASD · Space handbrake · **mouse free-look** · **LMB drive-by** · **N radio** · F hook/release *(wrecker only)* · R reset · Tab switch vehicle *(your truck only)* · C camera · T hot-reload tuning
**On foot:** **E take the nearest car** · **G interact** (vending machines, ATMs) · WASD walk · Shift sprint · Space jump · **CTRL crouch** · walk into a low wall to **mantle** · mouse look · **Q switch weapon** (fists / pistol / shotgun / SMG / bat) · **RMB aim or guard · LMB fire or punch · R reload** · ESC free the mouse
*The prompt above your hands tells you what E will do and whether it's a crime (orange = somebody's watching).*
**Headless smoke test:** `cd game && /Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --headless -- --smoke`
**Tune the feel:** edit `game/data/vehicles/*.json` (see [docs/tech/physics/tuning-guide.md](docs/tech/physics/tuning-guide.md)), press T in-game
